# Code of Conduct

Q-agent is a learning environment as well as an open-source project. Everyone
participating in issues, discussions, reviews, course activities, and related
community spaces is expected to help make it respectful and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific when giving feedback.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Credit collaborators and data or research sources.
- Respect privacy, licensing, academic-integrity rules, and course policies.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and repository standards.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private information, plagiarism, and repeated
disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Course participants should report conduct concerns to the instructor through
the course's official private channel. Other contributors may use the
[private repository reporting form](https://github.com/WolfpackOfOne/Q-agent/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the title with `Conduct report`. Do not post sensitive conduct reports
in public issues or discussions.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban a
participant when behavior is unsafe or persistently disruptive. Reports will be
handled as privately as practical, consistent with safety and institutional
requirements.
